24 sessions · 12 weeks · two hours each

All lessons

Every session has a page for the student and a run-sheet for the teacher. Sessions marked with a yellow edge are timed checkpoints, where the game layer is switched off entirely.

Week 1 — Orientation

2 sessions
Session A · Finding the subject Reading & writing — Strip a sentence back to its subject and verb by crossing out the parts that are only describing. Establish the error log and the confidence habit. Open the lesson Teacher sheet
Session B · Linear equations, and what slope means Mathematics — Solve linear equations reliably, and read slope and intercept as meaning rather than as letters in a formula. Open the lesson Teacher sheet

Week 2 — Agreement

2 sessions
Session A · Making the verb match Reading & writing — Find the true subject through interrupting phrases, and match the verb to it every time. Open the lesson Teacher sheet
Session B · Turning words into equations Mathematics — Convert a worded situation into an equation or inequality without losing a condition. Open the lesson Teacher sheet

Week 3 — Sentence boundaries

2 sessions
Session A · Where one sentence ends Reading & writing — Test any string of words for whether it is a complete sentence, and join two of them legally. Open the lesson Teacher sheet
Session B · Ratios, rates and percentages Mathematics — Set up and solve proportion, rate and percentage-change problems without second-guessing the direction. Open the lesson Teacher sheet

Week 4 — Checkpoint 1

2 sessions
Session A · Practice test 1 — reading and writing Checkpoint 1 — A clean, properly timed section score, and an error log broken down by domain rather than by question number. Open the timer Teacher sheet
Session B · Practice test 1 — mathematics Checkpoint 1 — A clean maths section score and a domain tally: algebra, data, geometry, advanced. Open the timer Teacher sheet

Week 5 — Punctuation and data

2 sessions
Session A · Commas, dashes and colons Reading & writing — Choose punctuation by structure rather than by pause, and know what a colon and a dash each require to their left. Open the lesson Teacher sheet
Session B · Reading tables and graphs Mathematics — Pull the right number out of a table or chart, and handle mean, median and two-way tables. Open the lesson Teacher sheet

Week 6 — Apostrophes and geometry

2 sessions
Session A · Apostrophes and pronouns Reading & writing — Distinguish its/it's and their/they're/there reliably, and spot a pronoun with no clear antecedent. Open the lesson Teacher sheet
Session B · Angles, triangles and the unit circle Mathematics — Use the angle and triangle rules confidently, and read the unit circle rather than memorise it. Open the lesson Teacher sheet

Week 7 — Tenses and advanced maths

2 sessions
Session A · Tenses and misplaced descriptions Reading & writing — Choose a verb form from the passage's existing timeline, and detect a dangling modifier by asking who is doing the action. Open the lesson Teacher sheet
Session B · Quadratics and functions Mathematics — Handle function notation, factorise a quadratic, and read what a parabola's features mean. Open the lesson Teacher sheet

Week 8 — Checkpoint 2

2 sessions
Session A · Practice test 2 — reading and writing Checkpoint 2 — Measure movement since checkpoint 1, especially in grammar rules — the area that should have moved most. Open the timer Teacher sheet
Session B · Practice test 2 — mathematics Checkpoint 2 — Measure movement across all four maths domains, and get a first honest reading on advanced maths. Open the timer Teacher sheet

Week 9 — Transitions and repair

2 sessions
Session A · Joining ideas Reading & writing — Read the relationship between two sentences before looking at any choice, then pick the transition that matches it. Open the lesson Teacher sheet
Session B · Repair session Mathematics — Close the largest gap that checkpoint 2 revealed, or continue advanced maths if nothing more urgent surfaced. Open the lesson Teacher sheet

Week 10 — Rhetoric and exam craft

2 sessions
Session A · Purpose and synthesis Reading & writing — Answer a purpose question from the passage's structure, and handle rhetorical synthesis by reading the goal first. Open the lesson Teacher sheet
Session B · Exam craft Mathematics — Recognise what a question is before solving it, and use backsolving, number-picking and the built-in calculator deliberately. Open the lesson Teacher sheet

Week 11 — Timed drills

2 sessions
Session A · Test 3 — one reading and writing module Timed drill — Practise pacing under real timing, then review every question — including the correct ones. Open the timer Teacher sheet
Session B · Test 3 — one mathematics module Timed drill — Practise maths pacing, and build the habit of cutting losses. Open the timer Teacher sheet

Week 12 — Final

2 sessions
Session A · Practice test 4 — reading and writing Final — A final honest section score, and a three-point trend line across weeks 4, 8 and 12. Open the timer Teacher sheet
Session B · Practice test 4 — mathematics, and the exam-day plan Final — Final maths score, and an agreed, written plan for exam day. Open the timer Teacher sheet
