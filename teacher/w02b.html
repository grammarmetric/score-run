Teacher sheet · week 2 · session B · Mathematics

Turning words into equations

Systems and inequalities, but the real target is translation — the point where the reading weakness starts costing maths marks.

Goal for this session

Convert a worded situation into an equation or inequality without losing a condition.

PPP — how the lesson page is structured

Present Warm-up + 3 teaching modulesOpens with the “The bridge from words to symbols” analogy, then teaches: Check the direction with real numbers · Inequality words · Solving a system, then answering the right part. Each module carries concept cards, a right/wrong example pair, the rule, the trap the exam builds from it, and a worked example in numbered steps. Nothing is timed and nothing costs a life.
Practice Recon, then waves3 recon questions where they label why each wrong option is wrong, then 8 timed questions at 95s each. Scaffolded: hints, a scanner perk, and shields from streaks.
Produce Boss5 questions, no hints and no scaffolding, with HP carried over from practice. This is the independent-application stage — do not coach through it.

If they lose all HP the run ends and restarts from the briefing. That is deliberate: a failed run costs about four minutes and they will usually go again without being asked.

What to watch for

  • The "twice as many" reversal. If it appears twice, stop and drill it in isolation — it will otherwise recur every week to week 12.
  • Whether they read the final sentence of the question before setting up.

Errors to expect

  • Reversing the ratio in a translation.
  • Using > when the wording says "at least".
  • Solving the system correctly and then answering for the wrong variable.

Homework to set

  • Kaplan Advanced Prep for 1600 ch 11 remaining drills, PDF p. 260–272
  • Rewrite three missed questions as a plain-English sentence first, then as an equation
