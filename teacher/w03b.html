Teacher sheet · week 3 · session B · Mathematics

Ratios, rates and percentages

The mechanical half of the weakest area on the diagnostic — 1 correct out of 7. Start here, because these can be made reliable quickly.

Goal for this session

Set up and solve proportion, rate and percentage-change problems without second-guessing the direction.

PPP — how the lesson page is structured

Present Warm-up + 3 teaching modulesOpens with the “Everything is measured against the original” analogy, then teaches: Percentage of, versus percentage change · Rates carry units · Ratios: find one part first. Each module carries concept cards, a right/wrong example pair, the rule, the trap the exam builds from it, and a worked example in numbered steps. Nothing is timed and nothing costs a life.
Practice Recon, then waves3 recon questions where they label why each wrong option is wrong, then 8 timed questions at 95s each. Scaffolded: hints, a scanner perk, and shields from streaks.
Produce Boss5 questions, no hints and no scaffolding, with HP carried over from practice. This is the independent-application stage — do not coach through it.

If they lose all HP the run ends and restarts from the briefing. That is deliberate: a failed run costs about four minutes and they will usually go again without being asked.

What to watch for

  • Percentage of versus percentage change — if these blur, everything in this domain fails.
  • Whether units get carried through. Most rate errors here are unit errors.

Errors to expect

  • Finding the new value when the question asked for the increase.
  • Averaging two rates directly instead of using total over total.
  • Applying a percentage to the wrong base.
