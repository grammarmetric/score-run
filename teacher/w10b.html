Teacher sheet · week 10 · session B · Mathematics

Exam craft

The techniques that only pay once the content is there — which, by week 10, it is. Mixed questions with no topic labels.

Goal for this session

Recognise what a question is before solving it, and use backsolving, number-picking and the built-in calculator deliberately.

PPP — how the lesson page is structured

Present BriefingThe rule is taught and one worked example is walked through line by line, then a free question that costs nothing. No timer, no HP at risk. Rule as the student sees it: “Recognise the question type before solving. If the answers are numbers, work backwards; if they are expressions, pick a value.”
Practice Recon, then waves3 recon questions where they label why each wrong option is wrong, then 8 timed questions at 95s each. Scaffolded: hints, a scanner perk, and shields from streaks.
Produce Boss5 questions, no hints and no scaffolding, with HP carried over from practice. This is the independent-application stage — do not coach through it.

If they lose all HP the run ends and restarts from the briefing. That is deliberate: a failed run costs about four minutes and they will usually go again without being asked.

What to watch for

  • Whether they can name the topic of a question before starting it. That recognition step is what the mixed set is testing.
  • Over-use of backsolving on questions where solving directly is faster.

Errors to expect

  • Backsolving from A instead of the middle.
  • Picking 0 or 1 when the question makes them special cases.
  • Trusting the calculator on a question that needed rearranging first.

Homework to set

  • Mixed set, timed, unlabelled
