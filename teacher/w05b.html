Teacher sheet · week 5 · session B · Mathematics

Reading tables and graphs

The interpretation half of problem solving and data — where the reading weakness meets the maths paper most directly.

Goal for this session

Pull the right number out of a table or chart, and handle mean, median and two-way tables.

PPP — how the lesson page is structured

Present Warm-up + 3 teaching modulesOpens with the “Read the instrument before the question” analogy, then teaches: The four-second routine · Two-way tables: the words pick the denominator · Mean, median and mode. Each module carries concept cards, a right/wrong example pair, the rule, the trap the exam builds from it, and a worked example in numbered steps. Nothing is timed and nothing costs a life.
Practice Recon, then waves3 recon questions where they label why each wrong option is wrong, then 8 timed questions at 95s each. Scaffolded: hints, a scanner perk, and shields from streaks.
Produce Boss5 questions, no hints and no scaffolding, with HP carried over from practice. This is the independent-application stage — do not coach through it.

If they lose all HP the run ends and restarts from the briefing. That is deliberate: a failed run costs about four minutes and they will usually go again without being asked.

What to watch for

  • Reading the wrong row or column — usually a rushing error, not a comprehension one.
  • Mean and median being treated as interchangeable.

Errors to expect

  • Using the total row as a data row in a two-way table.
  • Finding the median without ordering the list first.
  • Missing that an axis does not start at zero.
