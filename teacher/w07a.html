Tenses and misplaced descriptions

Keeping time consistent across a passage, and making sure a describing phrase is next to the thing it describes.

Goal for this session

Choose a verb form from the passage's existing timeline, and detect a dangling modifier by asking who is doing the action.

What you need open

College Panda SAT Writing — ch 15 tenses, PDF p. 79–84
College Panda SAT Writing — ch 7 modifiers, PDF p. 28–33

Running order

0:00 Retrieval warm-upTen pronoun and apostrophe items from week 6.
0:15 Tense by evidenceThe right tense is never a matter of taste — there is always another verb or a time marker in the passage that settles it. Find the evidence, then choose.
0:30 The modifier questionAfter an opening phrase, ask: who or what is doing this? The answer must be the very next noun. One question, applied every time.
0:40 Trap forensicsPhase 1.

What to watch for

  • Tense chosen by what sounds natural in speech rather than by the passage timeline.
  • Dangling modifiers being read as fine because the meaning is recoverable.

Errors to expect

  • Switching tense mid-passage.
  • Using the past perfect where the simple past is correct.
  • An opening phrase describing the wrong noun.

Homework to set

  • College Panda SAT Writing ch 15 and ch 7 exercises
