Quadratics and functions

The topic the diagnostic never measured — and roughly a third of the real maths section. Teach it assuming nothing.

Goal for this session

Handle function notation, factorise a quadratic, and read what a parabola's features mean.

What you need open

Kaplan Advanced Prep for 1600 — ch 13 passport to advanced math, PDF p. 317–340

What to watch for

  • Whether f(x) notation is understood at all. If not, this becomes a two-week topic and week 9 session B absorbs the overflow.
  • Confidence collapsing on sight of a quadratic. Watch for skipping before reading.

Errors to expect

  • Reading f(3) as f times 3.
  • Losing a sign when factorising.
  • Giving the x-intercepts when the vertex was asked for.
