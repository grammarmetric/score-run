Teacher sheet · week 7 · session B · Mathematics

Quadratics and functions

The topic the diagnostic never measured — and roughly a third of the real maths section. Teach it assuming nothing.

Goal for this session

Handle function notation, factorise a quadratic, and read what a parabola's features mean.

PPP — how the lesson page is structured

Present Warm-up + 3 teaching modulesOpens with the “A machine, not a multiplication” analogy, then teaches: f(x) is an instruction · Factorising, and keeping both roots · Vertex form, and the sign that flips. Each module carries concept cards, a right/wrong example pair, the rule, the trap the exam builds from it, and a worked example in numbered steps. Nothing is timed and nothing costs a life.
Practice Recon, then waves3 recon questions where they label why each wrong option is wrong, then 8 timed questions at 95s each. Scaffolded: hints, a scanner perk, and shields from streaks.
Produce Boss5 questions, no hints and no scaffolding, with HP carried over from practice. This is the independent-application stage — do not coach through it.

If they lose all HP the run ends and restarts from the briefing. That is deliberate: a failed run costs about four minutes and they will usually go again without being asked.

What to watch for

  • Whether f(x) notation is understood at all. If not, this becomes a two-week topic and week 9 session B absorbs the overflow.
  • Confidence collapsing on sight of a quadratic. Watch for skipping before reading.

Errors to expect

  • Reading f(3) as f times 3.
  • Losing a sign when factorising.
  • Giving the x-intercepts when the vertex was asked for.
