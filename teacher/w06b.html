Teacher sheet · week 6 · session B · Mathematics

Angles, triangles and the unit circle

Geometry is a small section and largely formula-driven — a contained fix. There is a purpose-built interactive lesson for the second half.

Goal for this session

Use the angle and triangle rules confidently, and read the unit circle rather than memorise it.

PPP — how the lesson page is structured

Present Warm-up + 3 teaching modulesOpens with the “A short list, known cold” analogy, then teaches: The angle rules · Pythagoras, and the triples worth memorising · The trigonometry worth knowing by heart. Each module carries concept cards, a right/wrong example pair, the rule, the trap the exam builds from it, and a worked example in numbered steps. Nothing is timed and nothing costs a life.
Practice Recon, then waves3 recon questions where they label why each wrong option is wrong, then 8 timed questions at 95s each. Scaffolded: hints, a scanner perk, and shields from streaks.
Produce Boss5 questions, no hints and no scaffolding, with HP carried over from practice. This is the independent-application stage — do not coach through it.

If they lose all HP the run ends and restarts from the briefing. That is deliberate: a failed run costs about four minutes and they will usually go again without being asked.

What to watch for

  • Whether the formula sheet is being used at all — it is provided on the real exam and many students forget it exists.
  • Degrees and radians being mixed.

Errors to expect

  • Assuming a triangle is right-angled because it looks it.
  • Using the wrong pair of sides in a ratio.
  • Forgetting that angles on a straight line sum to 180.
