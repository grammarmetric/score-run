Angles, triangles and the unit circle

Geometry is a small section and largely formula-driven — a contained fix. There is a purpose-built interactive lesson for the second half.

Goal for this session

Use the angle and triangle rules confidently, and read the unit circle rather than memorise it.

What to watch for

  • Whether the formula sheet is being used at all — it is provided on the real exam and many students forget it exists.
  • Degrees and radians being mixed.

Errors to expect

  • Assuming a triangle is right-angled because it looks it.
  • Using the wrong pair of sides in a ratio.
  • Forgetting that angles on a straight line sum to 180.
